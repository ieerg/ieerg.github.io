Biographies

Galo Nuño Barrau
Full Professor · Monetary economics, macro-finance

Galo Nuño is Full Professor of Economics and Vice Dean of the IE School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs at IE University, which he joined in 2026. He holds a PhD from the Universidad Politécnica de Madrid and an MSc in Management Science and Engineering from Stanford University. Before joining IE he spent more than two decades in central banking, most recently at the Banco de España, where he was Director General for Institutional and European Relations and Transparency and, earlier, Associate Director General for Economics and Head of the Monetary Policy and Capital Markets Division, with previous positions at the BIS, the European Central Bank and BBVA. His research focuses on monetary economics, macro-finance and computational methods, including continuous-time heterogeneous-agent models and deep learning. He is a Research Fellow of CEPR and CESifo.

Daniel Fernández Kranz
Associate Professor · Labor, family and gender economics; policy evaluation

Daniel Fernández Kranz is Associate Professor of Economics at IE University, where he has led the Economics Department since 2012 and has been Vice Dean for Economics of the IE School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs since 2022. He holds a PhD in Economics from the University of Chicago (2000). Before joining IE in 2008 he was Associate Professor at Saint Louis University (Madrid campus) and an economic consultant at NERA, working on antitrust and competition cases. He is an empirical microeconomist working on the economics of the family, labor markets, gender, health and public policy evaluation, and is a Research Fellow of IZA.

Liliana Gelabert
Associate Professor · Innovation and environmental economics

Liliana Gelabert is Associate Professor in the Economics Department at IE University, which she joined in 2009. She holds a PhD in Business Economics and Quantitative Methods from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (2009) and a degree in Economics from the Universidad de la República (Uruguay), and was a visiting researcher at the Economics of Climate Change Chair at FEDEA. Her research is in applied microeconomics, focusing on the economics of innovation, in particular public support for R&D, and on environmental economics, including corporate environmental strategy and urban sustainability. Her paper on localizing the Sustainable Development Goals won the Academy of Management Discoveries 2020 Best Paper Award.

Patricia Gabaldón
Associate Professor · Gender economics, corporate governance

Patricia Gabaldón is Associate Professor in the Economics Department at IE University, which she joined in 2008. She holds a PhD in Economics and Business from the Universidad de Alcalá and was a Visiting Research Scholar at Harvard University in 2012; she previously served as Research Director of IE Business School. Her research combines economics and sociology to study gender issues, in particular women on corporate boards, board gender quotas, female entrepreneurship and the governance of sustainable corporate behaviour, and has appeared in journals such as the Journal of Business Ethics, Corporate Governance: An International Review and the British Journal of Management.

Sasha Cheipesh
Postdoctoral Fellow · Health and labor economics

Oleksandra (Sasha) Cheipesh joined the Economics Department at IE University in 2026 after completing her PhD in Economics at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid, where she received the Excellence in Teaching Award in 2022, 2023 and 2026. She was a visiting student at Yale University in 2024 and holds an MS in Economics from the University of Vienna. Her research is in applied economics at the intersection of health economics, labor economics and gender norms, studying how health, public policy and gender norms shape labor-market outcomes and human capital.

Toni Roldán
Assistant Professor · Education economics, AI and labor markets

Toni Roldán Monés is Assistant Professor of Economics at IE University's School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs, which he joined in January 2026. He holds a PhD in Social Policy from the London School of Economics (2024), an MPhil in Political Economy from the LSE European Institute and an MPA in Economic Policy Management from Columbia University. He founded and directed the Esade Centre for Economic Policy (EsadeEcPol, 2019–2025), was a Visiting Professor in Practice at the LSE School of Public Policy and served as a Member of the Spanish Parliament (2015–2019). His experimental research focuses on the impact of technology, including generative AI, on inequality, education and productivity, and on evidence-based policy adoption. He is a regular columnist for El País.

Lorenzo Sileci
Assistant Professor · Environmental and development economics

Lorenzo Sileci is an Assistant Professor of Economics at IE University, which he joined in September 2025. He holds a PhD in Environmental Economics from the London School of Economics (2023) and was previously a Postdoctoral Research Officer at the Grantham Research Institute on Climate Change and the Environment at LSE, where he remains a Visiting Research Fellow. His research lies at the intersection of environmental and development economics, using applied econometrics, georeferenced data and satellite imagery to study climate mitigation, forests and carbon, biodiversity, and the environment–development trade-offs of extractive industries.

Mattia Fracchia
Assistant Professor · Development, behavioral and health economics

Mattia Fracchia is an Assistant Professor of Economics at IE University, which he joined in 2024. He holds a PhD in Economics and Finance from Nova School of Business and Economics (2024), where his dissertation received the Amélia de Mello Foundation Best PhD Dissertation Award, and an MSc in Economic Analysis from the Barcelona School of Economics. He is an external member of NOVAFRICA and an invited researcher at J-PAL. His research is in applied microeconomics, spanning development, behavioral, personnel and health economics, with extensive field experiments in Mozambique and Guinea-Bissau.

Ghassane Benmir
Assistant Professor · Macroeconomics, finance and climate economics

Ghassane Benmir is an Assistant Professor of Economics at IE University, which he joined in September 2023. He holds PhDs from the London School of Economics (Essays in Macro-Finance and Climate Change) and from PSL Research University – Université Paris Dauphine (Essays in Macroeconomics), both completed in 2023, and has been a guest consultant at the European Central Bank's Monetary Policy Research Directorate. His research combines macroeconomics, macro-finance and climate economics, with a focus on heterogeneous-agent models, asset pricing and the distributional effects of climate damages and environmental policy. His work received the European Finance Association Best Paper Prize in Responsible Finance (2021).

Siqi Wei
Assistant Professor · Applied econometrics; income and health risks

Siqi Wei is an Assistant Professor in the Economics Department at IE University, which she joined in 2022. She holds a PhD in Economics from CEMFI (2022), where her thesis on income, employment and health risks was supervised by Manuel Arellano and received the Enrique Fuentes Quintana Prize (FUNCAS). Her research is in applied econometrics, with a focus on the income, health and unemployment risks individuals face, income and health dynamics, and latent-variable panel data models.

Ainara González de San Román
Assistant Professor · Gender, education and labor markets

Ainara González de San Román is an Assistant Professor in the Economics Department at IE University, which she joined in 2013, where she is Coordinator of Data Analysis for Economics and Director of the Econ Data Lab. She holds a PhD in Economics from the Universidad del País Vasco (2013) and an MPhil in Economics and Finance from CEMFI (2009).
