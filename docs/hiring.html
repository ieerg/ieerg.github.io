We Are Hiring

The Department of Economics at IE University is growing. This academic year we are recruiting three tenure-track faculty members in macroeconomics, monetary economics and time-series econometrics, to join the IE Economics Research Group in September 2027.

Tenure-Track Faculty Positions in Economics 2026-2027

  • Rank: Assistant or Associate Professor (tenure track)
  • Fields: Macroeconomics, Monetary Economics, Time-series Econometrics
  • Start date: September 1, 2027
  • Location: IE School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs, Madrid, Spain
  • Application deadline: November 6, 2026
  • Interviews: European Job Market virtual meetings, December 14–17, 2026

IE University is looking for applicants in macroeconomics to fill three tenure-track positions as Assistant or Associate Professor in the Department of Economics, starting September 1, 2027. Position duties include publishing in leading economic journals, teaching at the undergraduate and/or graduate levels, and contributing to the intellectual life of the School.

We are looking for candidates in Macroeconomics, Monetary Economics, or Time-series Econometrics.

Candidates are offered a competitive remuneration package, a dynamic research environment, and a cosmopolitan workplace in the heart of Madrid. English is the working language, and our faculty and students are highly diverse and international. Knowledge of Spanish is not required.

Applicants should have completed or be close to completing a Ph.D. in Economics, Finance, or a related field from an internationally recognized institution by July 2027.

Application Instructions

Interested applicants should submit their materials through Interfolio by November 6, 2026. Applications should include:

  • A cover letter
  • Curriculum vitae
  • Job market paper
  • Three confidential letters of recommendation

Interviews will be conducted through the European Job Market virtual meetings held from the 14th to the 17th of December 2026.

For any questions regarding the application process, please contact Sara.Flores@ie.edu.

IE University is an Equal Opportunity Employer and strongly encourages applications from individuals who will enhance the ethnic and gender diversity of our faculty.
